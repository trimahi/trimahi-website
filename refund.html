Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective from 17 November 2026

Digital products

Because digital products can be downloaded and copied as soon as they are delivered, all sales of digital products are final and orders cannot be cancelled after delivery. We will give a full refund in these cases:

  • You were charged but did not receive the product within 48 hours, and we cannot deliver it after you contact us
  • You were charged twice for the same order
  • The file is damaged or does not open, and we cannot send a working replacement within 3 business days

To request a refund, contact us within 7 days of purchase with your order or payment ID.

Services (3D renders, videos, content, design)

  • Before work starts: you can cancel and get a full refund of your advance.
  • After work starts: the advance covers work already done and is not refundable. If you cancel midway, you pay only for the completed portion and receive those files.
  • Revisions: each order includes the number of revisions stated in the quote (usually 2). Dissatisfaction after the agreed revisions is not a ground for a refund.
  • Monthly packages: you can cancel with notice before the next billing date. The current month is not refundable.

Construction and interior projects

Cancellation terms for construction and interior projects are set out in the signed quotation or agreement for that project. Materials already bought or work already done are not refundable.

How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval.
  • Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show the credit.
  • We will confirm approval or rejection of your request within 3 business days.

How to request a refund or cancellation

Email hello@trimahi.homes or WhatsApp +91 91026 32666 with your name, order or payment ID and the reason.
