Frequently asked questions

Is the per sq ft rate final?

The rate gives you a reliable budget. The final quote comes after a site visit, your naksha and a detailed BOQ that lists every material and brand.

Which cities do you work in?

We take projects across India. Rates for your city are confirmed after a site visit.

Can you help with the naksha?

Yes. We can review your naksha, suggest Vastu-conscious changes and show you a 3D view of the elevation before construction.

Can I choose the brands of materials?

Yes. Cement, steel, tiles, paint, wires and fittings can be chosen by you. The BOQ shows each choice and its cost.

How do I get started?

Send your plot size, location and the number of floors on WhatsApp. We will call back with a first estimate and fix a site visit.
