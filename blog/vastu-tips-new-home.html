Vastu tips for a new home: 7 practical points for your naksha

By Trimahi Developers & Interiors · 17 November 2026

Vastu works best when it is planned into the naksha from the start. These seven points cover the decisions most families ask about, and each one also supports good light and ventilation.

1. Main entrance

North and east facing entrances are generally preferred. Keep the entrance well lit, clean and free of obstacles.

2. Kitchen in the south-east

The south-east (Agni corner) is the traditional place for the kitchen. North-west is the usual second choice.

3. Master bedroom in the south-west

The south-west corner is considered stable and suits the head of the family. Keep heavy furniture against the south and west walls.

4. Puja room in the north-east

The north-east (Ishan corner) is kept light, open and clean, which makes it ideal for a puja room or meditation space.

5. Toilets away from the north-east

Avoid toilets in the north-east and the centre of the house. West and north-west are commonly used.

6. Staircase in the south or west

Staircases are usually placed in the south, west or south-west, and they should not start from the centre of the house.

7. Keep the centre open

The centre of the house (Brahmasthan) is kept free of heavy walls, toilets and staircases where possible.

Vastu and practical design together

Every plot is different, so road direction, plot shape and local rules decide what is possible. A good naksha balances Vastu with light, air, privacy and budget. Trimahi reviews your naksha with both in mind.
