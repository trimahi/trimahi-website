House construction cost in India (2026): rates per sq ft explained

By Trimahi Developers & Interiors · 17 November 2026

Building a complete house with Trimahi starts at about ₹1,700 to ₹3,200 per sq ft of built-up area in 2026, depending on the quality of materials and finishes you choose. Rates vary by city and site.

Construction rates per sq ft

PackageBest forRate
BasicRental floors, tight budgets₹1,700 /sq ft
StandardMost family homes₹2,400 /sq ft
PremiumBranded materials, premium finishes₹3,200 /sq ft

These rates cover the full construction of the building. Land, boundary wall, borewell and approvals are usually counted separately.

Worked examples

Built-up area is the area of each floor added together. A G+1 house with 1,000 sq ft on each floor has 2,000 sq ft of built-up area.

What changes the cost

  • Material brands: cement, steel, tiles, sanitary fittings, wires and paint make the biggest difference between packages.
  • Design: large balconies, elevation features, double-height spaces and cantilevers add cost.
  • Soil and foundation: weak or filled-up soil may need a deeper or stronger foundation.
  • Number of floors: the foundation and columns must be designed for every future floor, even if you build only the ground floor now.
  • City and site access: labour and material prices differ between cities, and narrow lanes increase transport costs.

How to keep your budget under control

  1. Finalise the naksha and 3D view before construction starts. Changes during construction cost the most.
  2. Ask for a detailed BOQ that lists every material, its brand and its rate.
  3. Spend on the structure first: steel, cement and waterproofing. Finishes can be upgraded later.
  4. Keep 5–10% of your budget aside for surprises.
