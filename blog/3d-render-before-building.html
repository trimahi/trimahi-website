Why you should see a 3D render before building your house

By Trimahi Developers & Interiors · 17 November 2026

A 3D render is a realistic picture of your house, made from your 2D naksha before construction starts. It lets you see your home and fix problems while they are still free to fix.

What a 3D render shows

  • The front elevation, with colours, textures and materials
  • How the house looks in daylight from the street
  • Rooms with furniture, lighting and finishes
  • How balconies, windows and the parapet look together

Five reasons to get one before you build

  1. Avoid expensive changes. Breaking and rebuilding an elevation feature costs far more than changing a drawing.
  2. Choose colours with confidence. Compare two or three colour schemes before buying paint and tiles.
  3. Get the family to agree. Everyone understands a picture, and not everyone can read a naksha.
  4. Plan furniture early. Interior renders show whether the bed, wardrobe and sofa really fit.
  5. Sell or rent faster. Builders use renders to book flats and shops before they are finished.
