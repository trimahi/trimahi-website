Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective from 17 November 2026

Digital products

  • We sell digital products only. No physical item is shipped and there are no shipping charges.
  • After successful payment, the product is delivered to the email and WhatsApp number you give at checkout, within 24 hours (usually much sooner).
  • If you have not received it within 24 hours, check your spam folder and then contact us. We will resend it.

Services

  • 3D renders, staging, videos and content are delivered as digital files by Google Drive link, email or WhatsApp.
  • Delivery timelines are those stated on our website or in your quote (for example, 24–48 hours for most renders), counted from receipt of the advance and all required inputs.

Construction and interior projects

Timelines for on-site work are agreed in the quotation or agreement for each project.

Service area

Digital products and AI Studio services are available across India. Customers outside India can contact us for international orders.
