Privacy Policy

Effective from 17 November 2026

This policy explains how Trimahi Developers & Interiors collects and uses your personal data when you use trimahi.homes, contact us or buy from us. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian laws.

1. Data we collect

  • Data you give us: name, phone number, email, city, and project details you share through forms, WhatsApp, calls or email.
  • Order data: the products or services you buy, invoice details and payment status. Card, UPI and bank details are handled only by our payment gateway, not by us.
  • Files you send: plans, photos and drawings shared for a project.
  • Basic website data: our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and performance.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and prepare quotes
  • To process orders, deliver digital products and provide services
  • To send invoices and order updates
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal data. We send marketing messages only if you agree, and you can opt out at any time.

3. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run our business, such as our payment gateway, hosting provider, email and WhatsApp, or when required by law.

4. Storage and security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law (for example, tax records). We use reasonable security measures to protect it.

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to hello@trimahi.homes. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. Third-party features such as embedded Google Maps may set their own cookies under their policies.
